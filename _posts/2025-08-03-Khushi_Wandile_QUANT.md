### Basic Information

- **Company Name**: Goldman Sachs
- **Name**: Khushi Wandile
- **Role**: Quant Analyst
- **Location**: Hyderabad 

### Please elaborate on your internship role and/or any projects that you did?

The role was of LFT (low frequency trading) in nature. Building/ benchmarking models (pricing models). Lot of ML concepts utilised.

### Please describe the selection process in detail (tests + interviews)?

1 test + 3 rounds of technical interviews 

### What was your preparation strategy for Quant roles (trading and/or software)?

Grip on Probability, statistics, ML, Maths and DSA concepts

### What were the working hours like?

7-8 hours

### How would you describe the structure, work culture and work-life balance of the company?

GS is culturally good and vibrant, many collaborative, social volunteering, well being and fun events throughout the year. Work-life balance is standard, team-to-team basis. People there are helpful and very approachable.

### A lot of Quant firms claim they don't have silos, and use this as a USP. How true is this for the company you interned at?

Yup no silos, in the experience I had :)

### Was there any evaluation criteria followed by the company, if yes could you elaborate?

Strongly based on how you approach and answer the questions in interviews

###  How did the internship influence your career interests? 

Gave me a good enough reason to stay in the domain I interned in.

9
