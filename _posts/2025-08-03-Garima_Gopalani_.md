### Selection process

### Road-blocks

Given the less amount of opportunities that open up for DD people, it was a bit disappointing at first. I even got rejected by a few firms. But constant motivation from friends and family helped :)

### Work during internship
I modelled a half duplex, synchronous communication link to achieve a very low bit error rate. It involved designing multiple components like transmitter-receiver, channel, PLL, etc


### Overall Internship Experience

TI has a very good work culture with the best part being it's people. Everyone was really helpful. Almost every evening after work, we used to go to Texins- which was a separate area with many sports facilities, music room, dance room, etc. It was a good experience overall!

### Networking opportunities

I made some really good connections with many people in my team including my mentor and manager. I also got lucky as I had multiple IITB alums working in my team. I bonded very well with them (over discussions like RR about some courses or profs XD)
