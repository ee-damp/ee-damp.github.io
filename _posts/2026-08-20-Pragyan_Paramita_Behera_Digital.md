### Domain Targeted
I wanted to go in digital field only… So Google, Nvidia and TI were mostly the companies I was targeting

I enjoyed Viru sir’s 224

### Preparation Resources
Past year intern questions along with the Digi question bank

Mostly the basics of digital electronics specifically EE224. And certain specific topics like STA and all which are mostly company specific. Might need to brush up VHDL, Verilog and C++ concepts along with a little DSA

### Interview Experience
It was mostly in the last week of Aug.. There was a short test and the shortlist came with 4-5 days and then was followed by the interview which was around 1hr 15 mins

Difficulty rating (1-5): 3

### Project Work
I worked on RAS Verification of the CPU

### Overall Experience
Work culture is great, you’ll meet great minds.. Most importantly those people are very supportive and helpful.

### Recommendation & Advice
I would definitely recommend this internship, you’ll get a good taste of corporate life. The way actually industry works and a lot more!
