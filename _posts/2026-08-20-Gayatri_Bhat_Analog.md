### Overall Experience
Overall experience was great. I have learnt a lot from internship. work culture was soo nice. All team members and any employee from google is friendly and helpful. I have made very good connection with co-interns, team members and out of team members as well. Food is so nice. There are many perks, including great food, great infrastructure, good networking, cabs service with safety even if u are going back late. Intern specific and overall fun events, sports competitions and mainly google tshirt, bags, water bottle, key chain and stickers

### Networking Opportunities
Yes, there are many opportunities for networking. There are cofee chats, many social events, competitions, some events for interns

### Recommendation & Advice
Yes, i will recommend this intership. Good learning, good networking is the main thing i would look for. Perks is also one thing and also stipend :D
