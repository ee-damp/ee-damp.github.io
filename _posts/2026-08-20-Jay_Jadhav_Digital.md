### Domain Targeted
I was aiming for core. Nothing in particular, just wanted to explore the core side of work.

I have a genuine interest in EVs and automobiles. Ig this role aligned with my interest.

### Preparation Resources
I mostly studied from the course content only. Like power engineering 1 and 2, singal processing and communication system, digital systems etc. nothing was there form outside for both test and interview.

Brush up in dc dc convertors, motors, and basics of digital signal Power engineering

### Interview Experience
Technical test -> technical interview 1-2 depending on the roles they want you to do for -> 1 HR interview at end

I got selected for interview in signals domain in TI, but I got rejected at the end of because it was my first interview of intern season. But i learned from the mistakes I made there.

Difficulty rating (1-5): 3

### Project Work
I was working on making digital model for the upcoming chetak vehicle. And making it useful for fault simulations

### Overall Experience
Work culture was nice. Manager was beyond chill guys. He didn't give any hard time. All the team members were very helpful all the way. Made many new friends in vo intern. Nice knowledge transfer happened there also.

### Networking Opportunities
Not like they made us do any particular networking. But I was free to talk to anyone I want to.

### Recommendation & Advice
I would recomend doing this intern. It gives s you a very food exposure to the real like backend work on the automobiles you sue in daily life. Very food company and nice projects if interested in automotive industry.
