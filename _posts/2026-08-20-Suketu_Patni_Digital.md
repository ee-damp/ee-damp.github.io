### Basic Information
- **Name**: Suketu Patni
- **Branch**: BTech
- **Company**: NVIDIA
- **Duration**: 9 weeks

### Domain Targeted
Mostly elec core and related (so like NVIDIA, TI, Samsung Korea, Sony)

I learnt a lot of digital design stuff over the summer. Then in my 5th semester I took VLSI design which I really liked and that sort of cemented my preference for elec core over anything else.

### Preparation Resources
Vatsal's question bank, Indranil Sengupta's youtube videos, HDLBits

Digital Systems, Microprocessors, single cycle/multi cycle/pipelined cpu are all important. basic vlsi design, bash scripting, and basic c/c++ is a plus.

### Interview Experience
technical test -> one interview (online but held in LA001) -> offer

Not during the selection process.

Difficulty rating (1-5): 4

### Project Work
RTL design of a safety related IP.

### Overall Experience
Amazing. Work culture is pretty healthy at NVIDIA with no fixed hours; its like everyone has their own schedule but they still finish the work on time. my manager and mentors were super nice and guided me a lot. i learnt a lot from them. i was lucky to get a project related to HDL; some other interns got verification (which would also have been good) but some got some stuff barely related to EE. facilities were great, we were kept in a hotel where we got breakfast and dinner and in the office we got breakfast, lunch, and snacks. workspace and work-related facilities were also top class.

### Networking Opportunities
Yeah, I am still going to be in touch with my mentors and my manager after my internship.

### Recommendation & Advice
100% recommended if you want some experience in digital design. this is pretty cutting edge work, and you get to learn a lot about how hardware is developed collaboratively, so i would definitely recommend it.
