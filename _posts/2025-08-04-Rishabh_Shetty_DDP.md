### Short Description of Project

High mobility communication suffers from inter-carrier interference which affects the orthogonality of subcarriers in OFDM, thereby increasing the transmission errors at the receiver. To mitigate this, various strategies like coded OFDM and OTFS are followed. My work is related to channel estimation under these scenarios which helps to increase the achievable rate in these transmission schemes
