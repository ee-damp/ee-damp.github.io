### Domain Targeted
AI ML RnD

### Preparation Resources
Should be well versed in various ML architectures - can be easily done using GfG and books like PRML - Pattern Recognition and Machine Learning. If you are doing a DS minor then you will easily have enough of a background. Just have 1-2 more projects of some sort done in ML. Some basic DSA should get you througb the coding part of the test

### Interview Experience
The interview was mostly based on ML projects in your resume. They would ask various questions about the ML arch involved and some other probability questions. Maybe expect a green book question in the last round. 

### Other Applications
nan

### Project Work
My project was purely research oriented in GNN RnD, the main aim was to figure out some mathematical framework of how certain things are happening and hopefully get a paper or a patent out. 

### Overall Experience
Quite amazing, it was not some dull report or ppt making, i read a lot of research papers and learnt a ton of new stuff. Got to experiment on a lot of things in order to figure out the new resaerch things, we filed a patent and started work on a resaech paper as well. 

### Organizational Culture
Was like a college group funnily(everyone got all the work done last minute but all the work was finished). My co-interns were quite amazing , i had a lot of fun with them and they were quite enthusiastic about teh research as well.

### Networking Opportunities
Yes the networking was quite great, my manager and buddy were quite enthusiastic to stay in touch

### PPO & Future Prospects
They are quite keen to distribute PPOs. Around 80% of the hiring pipeline is through intern PPOs.

### Recommendation & Advice
Yes, i would quite recommend this.
