### Short Description of Project

The project revolves around making the AI/ML/DL systems (1) more interpretable and (2) more economic and efficient. The problem of interpretability/explainability can range from trying to explain the behaviour of already built models (post-hoc) or designing models or frameworks which are inherently interpretable (ante-hoc). The problem of economy is mostly associated with reducing the number of trainable parameters for a task while maintaining the model performance. My work has revolved mostly around image generation models. The research focuses of exploring signal processing inspired ideas to achieve the aforementioned goals.
