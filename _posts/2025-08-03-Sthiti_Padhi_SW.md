### Preparation Resources
I prepared basic DSA (hashmaps, linked lists etc). I practiced questions from leetcode. I also read up embedded systems, implementing questions in C. 

### Interview Experience
For my interview, there were two questions in two rounds, each round lasting 45 mins.

1. The first question: There was a beaded necklace having arbitrary number of red and green beads. I had to divide them in two equal heaps. The question was based on sliding window approach.

2. The second question was based on hashmap implementation and bit wise operations. The question was long and took time to understand.

### Project Work

### Overall Experience
No, I learned a lot about threads and kernels among OS. Technical part aside, it was a good introduction to corporate workflow and review process. I also enjoyed brainstorming and exploring POC for different ideas.
