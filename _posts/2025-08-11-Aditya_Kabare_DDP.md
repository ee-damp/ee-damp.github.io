### Basic Information

- **Project Title / Domain**: Musical Analysis of Indian Drums

- **Guide**: Sibi Raj Pillai
- **Project Type**: theoretical

### Short Description of Project

Work has focused on the instrument Mridangam. We have looked at the modal analysis of the drumhead surface and the effects of the air column on it. Also, looking into modelling the propagation characteristics inside the drum.

### Whom did you work with?

PG students, The prof directly

### Tools / Simulation / Software / Hardware

Python for analysis/simulations, Audacity for audio recording and analysis
