### Project Work
I am a fourth-year student in the Electrical B.Tech program. I am a fellow who is open to exploring new opportunities that come up with an open mindset. I interned with P&G (Supply-Chain firm) with the operations team at one of their Plants situated at Bhopal.

P1) I was given a very open-ended problem statement to automate the troubleshooting /workpoint identification on production lines to reduce the time to find the workpoint and hence increase the throughput ( completed by preparing an automated data-fetching workflow followed by a RAG-based workpoint identification )

P2) I had to automate the evaluation-based promotion system in the firm by making tools that would help people to prepare these skills. This was basically RAG models hosted on their own ai tools based on Gemini Flash

### Recommendation & Advice
It might sound like a non-tech role in general, but as an intern, you get the role as per your resume, so join this firm with an open mindset. It is mostly about problem-solving, and you will get to learn a lot during your internship, something that you might not have explored so far.

The internship was going pretty smoothly, but there were two main challenges:
1) My model accuracy was poor mainly because of wrong data identification
2) Mid-review happened in the sixth week, so I was left with very less amount of time to prepare for my end-review while working on the feedback.
