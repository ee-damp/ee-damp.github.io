### Project Work
I worked on adding new features to the various testing frameworks for NVIDIA Video Drivers making them more robust and informative.

### Overall Experience
The internship experience was extremely great. The company is amazing and has a great culture. Yes, it is good-pay good-work desk-job, but it involved a lot of fun and didn't feel monotonous. The people there were helpful and amazing.

### Organizational Culture
The people are very helpful and soft-spoken, respecting everyone, working towards innovation. The company culture is great and supportive.

### Networking Opportunities
Yes, I have made connections with the teammates I worked with.

### PPO & Future Prospects
There are high chances of getting a PPO. They are willing to hire interns as FTEs. Also, NVIDIA rarely fires anyone once hired.

### Recommendation & Advice
Yes, I totally recommend any junior who wants to prepare for NVIDIA.
Additionally, as a tip, prepare for OS, memory allocation and networks basics for the interview.
