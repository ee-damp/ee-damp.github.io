### Selection process 
The technical test for TI was on August 23, 2024. It had four sections analog, digital, embedded and signal and we had to choose any two of them. I chose digital and embedded.
After the test, I was shortlisted for the embedded role interview, which happened on August 25, 2024. I received the offer on the same day through PT cell.

### Road-blocks

The hurdle for me was that I could not clear a software interview for which had prepared for immensely. 

### Work during internship
My project was to interface TI's corner radar sensor with their microcontroller to replace the existing pc based setup. So I had to build the entire flow from booting up the sensor, to configuring the sensor parameters then triggering the sensor and capturing the received data via CSI2 protocol.


### Overall Internship Experience

It was a good experience. I had a constant support from my mentor also our team had  weekly sync up meets to address the issues that the team was facing. 

### Networking opportunities

I was part of the Radar team, in the ADAS Software Apps subteam at TI. On my floor there were other subteams like Hardware Apps, SDK, HSM. I spoke with people from these teams to understand my project better and also got to know the projects they were working on. 

### Reccomended or not

This had a mix of both embedded software and a bit of hardware which made it more interesting for me.
My experience was great because I worked on a meaningful project that was useful for the team.
